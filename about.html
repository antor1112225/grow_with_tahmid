Who I am

I’m Tahmid — full name MD TAHMID HASAN ANTOR. I’m a performance marketer who specialises in one thing: using paid advertising to generate qualified leads and new customers for service businesses. Over the past four-plus years I have worked on more than 300 projects and managed over $5M in ad spend across Google Ads and Meta Ads.

What I specialise in

My work sits at the point where advertising, measurement and sales meet:

  • Google Ads — search and local campaigns that capture people actively looking for a service.
  • Meta Ads — Facebook and Instagram campaigns that create demand with the right offer and creative.
  • Conversion tracking — GA4, Google Tag Manager, Enhanced Conversions, Meta Pixel and Conversions API, and CRM imports.
  • Lead generation systems — landing pages, qualification and follow-up automation that turn enquiries into booked jobs.

Who I help

Most of my clients are businesses where a single new customer is valuable and buyers act with intent: home service companies (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, restoration, remodeling), automotive services (detailing, repair, collision, glass, towing), law firms, dental, medspa and healthcare clinics, and other high-intent B2C services such as real estate, mortgage, venues and care providers.

Why I focus on lead generation

Lead generation is where paid advertising is most measurable — you can see the call, the form, the booking and eventually the sale. It is also where poor setup is most expensive: when tracking counts page views as leads, or campaigns chase cheap clicks, businesses pay for activity that never turns into revenue. I would rather help a business get fewer, better leads it can actually close.

How I approach paid advertising

Intent before volume

A smaller number of leads from people who actually need the service beats a flood of cheap enquiries your team has to filter.

Measure what matters

Calls, forms and bookings are tracked as separate conversions, and qualified leads are fed back into the platforms whenever the CRM allows.

Small, documented changes

Weekly optimization with a written change log, so you can always see what changed, why, and what happened next.

Honest numbers

No vanity metrics and no inflated conversion counts. If something is not working, you hear it from me first.

Tracking and measurement

Google’s Smart Bidding and Meta’s delivery system optimise toward whatever you tell them is a conversion. So before I scale anything, I make sure the signal is right: each lead type tracked separately, duplicates removed, Enhanced Conversions and the Conversions API in place, and — wherever possible — qualified leads and closed jobs imported back from the CRM. Reporting then shows what you actually care about: spend, qualified leads, cost per lead and booked jobs. Learn how I set up conversion tracking.
