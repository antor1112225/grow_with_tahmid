GA4 & Google Tag Manager

GA4 & Google Tag Manager setups that answer real business questions

Clean analytics that answer business questions: where leads come from, what they do, and where they drop off.

Most GA4 properties collect a lot of data and answer very few questions. I set up GA4 around the decisions you need to make: which channels and campaigns generate leads, which pages convert, and where visitors drop off.

Google Tag Manager is organised with clear naming, a documented data layer and separate tags for GA4, Google Ads and Meta — so adding or auditing tracking later is simple rather than risky.

  • GA4 property & data stream configuration
  • Key events for calls, forms, bookings and chats
  • Consent mode set up with your cookie banner
  • Clean, documented GTM container
  • Looker Studio dashboard for owners

Landing Page / CRO

Landing pages built for one audience and one action

Convert more of the traffic you already pay for with pages built for one audience and one action.

Sending paid traffic to a homepage asks visitors to find their own way. A focused landing page matches the ad’s promise, answers the obvious questions, shows proof and makes the next step effortless — especially on mobile.

I write and structure landing pages (or brief your designer/developer), then test changes in order of expected impact: offer, headline, form length, proof and call-to-action placement.

  • Message match with ads and keywords
  • Short forms with the right qualifying questions
  • Click-to-call and sticky mobile actions
  • Speed and Core Web Vitals checks
  • Prioritised test roadmap

Remarketing / Retargeting

Remarketing that brings interested visitors back — politely

Bring back interested visitors who did not enquire the first time — at a fraction of the cost of new traffic.

Most people do not enquire on their first visit. Remarketing keeps you visible to people who already showed interest — viewed a service page, watched a video, opened a form — for a fraction of the cost of new traffic.

I build audiences by intent and recency, coordinate Google and Meta so people are not seeing the same message everywhere, and cap frequency so follow-up never feels like being followed.

  • Audiences by page, action and recency
  • Google Display, YouTube and Meta retargeting
  • Offer and proof-led follow-up creative
  • Frequency caps and exclusions for converted leads
  • Privacy-respecting audience setup

How I work

The same disciplined process for every service

  1. 01

    Discovery & Research

    We define what a qualified lead is worth to you, then I research search demand, competitors, your service area and your current account to find where money is leaking.

    OutcomeLead definition, budget estimate, audit findings

  2. 02

    Tracking & Campaign Setup

    Calls, forms and bookings are tracked as separate, verified conversions before a single dollar is spent. Campaigns are built around intent, location and offer.

    OutcomeVerified tracking, campaign structure, ads & landing page plan

  3. 03

    Launch & Data Collection

    Campaigns go live with controlled budgets. I watch search terms, lead quality and call recordings closely and remove waste early, while the platforms gather learning data.

    OutcomeLive campaigns, early waste removed, first lead-quality feedback

  4. 04

    Optimization & Scaling

    Budget shifts toward what produces qualified leads and booked jobs. Winning campaigns are scaled carefully; offline conversions teach bidding what a good lead looks like.

    OutcomeLower cost per qualified lead, documented changes, monthly review
