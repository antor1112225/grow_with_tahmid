01

Should I run Google Ads or Meta Ads for lead generation?

Google Ads captures people who are already actively searching for your service with high commercial intent, making it fastest for immediate phone calls and booked jobs. Meta Ads builds demand visually among people not yet searching, ideal for high-consideration, aesthetic, or visual services. Many businesses start with Google Search and expand into Meta once conversion tracking is proven.

02

How do Meta Ads leads differ from Google Ads leads?

Meta leads typically cost less per lead, but require stronger pre-qualification because users were casually scrolling rather than actively searching. We filter lead quality using custom intent forms, mandatory qualifying questions, automated instant SMS follow-ups, and Conversions API (CAPI) offline conversion feedback.

03

What makes a lead “qualified”?

We establish your exact qualification criteria before spending a single dollar: located inside your active service radius, looking for your most profitable services, with a realistic budget and immediate timeline. Our campaigns, landing pages, and smart bidding algorithms optimize exclusively toward these qualified prospects.

04

How much should I spend on ads?

Starting budgets depend on your local market competitiveness and average customer value. Most local service businesses see strong traction starting between $1,500 to $4,000/month in ad spend. During our initial strategy call, I analyze real auction CPC data and calculate your expected volume and cost per lead before you commit.

05

Why does conversion tracking matter so much?

Modern Smart Bidding optimizes for whatever actions you tell it to track. If page visits, accidental clicks, or spam submissions count as conversions, the algorithm wastes budget hunting for more unqualified traffic. Accurate call tracking, form submissions, and offline revenue synchronization ensure every dollar chases profitable customers.

06

How do you improve lead quality, not just lead volume?

By enforcing intent filters at every step: negative keyword lists that weed out job seekers and DIY research, strict geographic targeting, price anchors in ad copy, friction questions on lead forms, and syncing CRM closed-won data so algorithms prioritize paying clients.

07

How long until I see results?

Google Search campaigns often start generating phone calls and inbound inquiries within the first 7–14 days. Weeks 2 through 6 focus on eliminating search term waste and calibrating bidding. Accounts typically achieve consistent profitability and lower CPL by months 2 and 3.

08

What does working together look like?

It starts with a free audit and clear growth roadmap. Once onboarded, I handle full tracking setup, landing page recommendations, campaign build, and weekly proactive optimization. You receive transparent weekly reports, direct WhatsApp communication, and you always retain 100% ownership of your accounts.
